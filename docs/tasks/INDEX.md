Every task card, in build order. **Release 1 (Base and sign-in): 86 cards** (step 1.8, written 2 Nov 2026; the build plan of `docs/TECH-DESIGN.md` section 10, frozen with Release 1, D-191).

**How to pick a card:** do the first card whose status is **To do**, whose "Depends on" cards are all **Done**, and whose builder is you. Different builders may work on different cards at the same time, never on the same files (TECH-DESIGN section 9). **Step 2.1's 15 cards run strictly one after another**, because each edits files the next one needs.

**Full cards are written one step at a time.** Step 2.1's cards are written in full now (`docs/tasks/TASK-0001.md` to `TASK-0013.md`, and `TASK-0086.md` and `TASK-0087.md`, added by CR-010 and CR-011, with their tests). Each later step's cards, and their tests, are written in full by the planner before that step starts, from this list and the frozen design; a card that is not yet written in full cannot be started.

Status values: To do · In progress · Done · BLOCKED. "Part": Set-up (step 2.1 only), Backend or Frontend (D-187). Reviewer: never the builder (D-185).
