## Shared parts added in this batch

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Project cards** | A board card shows the stage bars (or the month's count for monthly work), the next date, the people, and a "monthly" tag. |
| **Comment pins** | Numbered pins on a design or page preview, each tied to a comment. |
| **Conversation** | Client messages on the left (WhatsApp green), ours on the right (dark), internal notes in a dashed amber box that clients never see, and grey system lines (flagged by AI, deadline passed). |
| **Deadline countdown** | Small pills: green when met, amber when due soon, red when late, grey for later dates. |
| **Week calendar** | All-day row (deadlines, milestones, invoices, leave, holidays) above a time grid; colour by type; a red line for now; holidays tinted. |
| **Post chips** | In the content calendar, a chip per post: platform letter badge, client initials, title, and a left edge coloured by status. |
| **Recording consent** | Recording for AI minutes starts only after everyone agrees; the agreement is shown with names and times. |
| **"Not for this service"** | Automations that a service does not use are shown greyed with the reason, so nothing is a surprise. |

### SCR-061 · Project

### SCR-065 · Support inbox and SCR-066 · Support request

- **Preview:** `support.html`, `support-request.html`
- **Inbox:** each request with channel, priority, person, **reply** and **fix** countdowns from the client's terms, status. A fix deadline on a Sunday moves to Monday (Kaveri's request from today: fix by Mon 5 Oct; choice 97, 22 Oct). A fixed request closes when the client says OK, or by itself 3 days after the fix if the client does not reply; the client can reopen it (choice 80, 22 Oct). Requests not covered by an AMC or free maintenance ask for a quote first.
- **From WhatsApp (D-161):** client messages go to their timelines; AI flags the ones that look like a problem, or matches them to an open request; a person makes the request with one tap. 14 other messages today went straight to timelines.
- **Request:** the whole conversation (WhatsApp, email, portal), internal notes, system lines; reply box with channel choice, ✦ draft reply and Tamil version; deadlines (met / late); details; links to the website, task, AMC and the login as a vault link.
- **Requirements:** PRJ-013, CON-014, AUTO-027, RULE-025, AI-009.

## Calendar and meetings

### SCR-085 · Meeting

- **Preview:** `meeting.html` (Kaveri homepage feedback) and `phone-work.html` (recording in progress)

- **Recording:** length, transcript, **everyone's agreement with names and times**, voice minutes used this month, kept until someone deletes it, with no automatic removal (choice 63, 22 Oct; also listed in Data, "Keeping data" tab).
- **Requirements:** CORE-050, CORE-051, D-160, AI-002, AI-006, AI-010.
