A landing page that doubled enquiries for a Trichy tuition centre

The problem

Parents could not find batch timings or fees online, so most enquiries came as phone calls in class hours, and many were missed.

What we did

One fast landing page with batch timings, fees and a WhatsApp enquiry button, plus a small Google ads campaign for "tuition near me" searches in Trichy.

Results in the first month

18 → 41enquiries a month
₹620 → ₹270cost per enquiry
2.1 spage load on a phone

"We got twice the enquiries in the first month, and parents can finally find our batch timings." · Mrs. Kavitha, founder
