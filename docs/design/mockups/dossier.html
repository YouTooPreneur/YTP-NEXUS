How it is built

  • WordPress 6.6 with Elementor; the car models list is a custom post type.
  • Hosting: YTP-VPS-01 (DigitalOcean), moved from YTP-HS-01 in May 2026 for speed.
  • Enquiry form: WPForms, sending through the Billing email sender; enquiries also go to the CRM.

DNS and email

  • Domain at the client's Namecheap; DNS at Cloudflare (YTP).
  • Client email is Zoho Mail. Do not change the MX or TXT records.
