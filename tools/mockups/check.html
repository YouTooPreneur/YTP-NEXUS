Screen check (D-181)

Loads every screen in docs/design/mockups/ in both colours (Emerald and Navy) at the design size (1600 × 1000), opens every tab, and lists any problem. Client and student portal pages and public link pages are also checked at phone width (390). Run it through the preview server: http://localhost:8765/tools/mockups/check.html, press Run all checks, and show a batch only with zero problems.

Without a window (the app's browser pane pauses hidden pages), run it headless, in parts: msedge --headless=new --disable-gpu --user-data-dir=<new temp folder> --virtual-time-budget=900000 --dump-dom "http://localhost:8765/tools/mockups/check.html?auto=1&from=0&to=40" and read the line CHECKED n pages · m problems.

What it looks for: script errors, icons that failed to draw, a page wider than its screen, content cut off at the bottom of a fixed (non-scrolling) page, text spilling out of its box, clipped button or heading text, overlapping buttons and chips, missing images, low text contrast, links to pages that don't exist, page styles that reuse menu and top-bar class names, leftover "comes in batch" notices, 12-digit numbers that look like a full Aadhaar, and 24-hour clock times (screens use 12-hour, 3:30 pm). New pages go in PAGES below; the check also lists any page in the folder that is missing from it.
